Cyberpunk: Edgerunners Characters

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners has a lot of interesting characters. Each character has their own personality, goals, and role in the story.

Main Characters

  • David Martinez
    • Edgerunner
    • Determined and ambitious
  • Lucy
    • Netrunner
    • Clever and resourceful
  • Rebecca
    • Fixer
    • Charismatic and manipulative
  • Maine
    • Edgerunner
    • Loyal and protective

Character Roles

Edgerunner
A mercenary who takes dangerous jobs in Night City.
Netrunner
A hacker who uses technology and cyberspace to accomplish jobs.
Fixer
A person who connects mercenaries with jobs and clients.

Why the Characters Are Important

The characters are one of my favorite parts of Edgerunners because they each have different personalities and goals. Their relationships also help make the story more interesting.
